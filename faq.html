Product and NFC/QR

How does a guest use the card?

By tapping an NFC-capable phone on it, or scanning the QR code with any camera. Both open the same page — no app.

Does it need an app or battery?

No. The card is passive: an NFC antenna plus print. The guest only needs a browser.

What if a phone has no NFC?

The QR back covers it — same URL, same menu, same reviews.

Customization

Can the card carry our branding?

Yes. The digital page updates centrally; every table follows without reprinting.
